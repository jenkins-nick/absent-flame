Where the stars fell

  • Twilight Gap the Ember

    A border pass, and the reason two armies were standing in the same mud at dusk.

  • Felwinter Peak the Dawn

    The last mountain still touched by real light. Three thousand people live at Anchor of Light because the sword stays put.

  • Hellmouth the Vigil

    A shaft punched clean through the world's crust. Past Sorrow's Harbor and the Circle of Bones, one white point in absolute dark.

  • Dust Palace the Feast

    A buried god's banquet hall under the frozen breadbasket, at the end of the trench the sword tore on its way down.

  • Six Fronts the Truce

    Named for the six armies that met here. Nobody agrees which one lost, which is part of why it has never stopped.

  • Kavzar the Hush

    The great capital that went quiet in a single night an age ago. Everyone knows the story. Nobody has ever gone to check it.

  • Harbinger's Seclude the Solitude

    A rock in the Ecliptic Drift. One cell, one occupant, and a last mile that can only be walked by one person at a time.

What the roads report

  • Frost line

    Cold that came with the dark and never lifted. It advances out of the northwest whether or not anyone fights it.

  • Fever ✕

    Old grudges reigniting for no reason at all. The marks cluster wherever there has recently been a fight.

  • Roads

    Dotted, and incomplete. Where a line stops, so did whoever reported it.

Dungeon Master only — what the map is lying about
  1. Kavzar is drawn as a ruin. The map is wrong in exactly the way every map in the world is wrong, which is the point.
  2. The tower is drawn as architecture. It has no foundation. It came down before the city, and the city was built around it.
  3. Nothing is marked in the southern basin — only an unexplained depression with no road to it. That is not an omission for the players' benefit. It is what every cartographer for an age actually did.
  4. The frost line and the fever are drawn as unrelated weather. By the end of the first session the characters can name four things going wrong. They have no idea they are looking at four people.
  5. Harbinger's Seclude is barely on the map at all. A rock, no port, no reason.
